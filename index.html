Reach the Flag

The level now has an end: a flagpole waiting down the road. New obstacles stop generating as you get close, so you get a clear final stretch. Touch the flag and the run stops, your stats pop up, and you can jump straight back in.

Controls

Spaceor↑— jump
→orD— sprint faster
←orA— ease off the pace

The run starts on its own. Click the stage once so it can hear your jumps. On a phone or tablet, on-screen buttons appear automatically.

Engine notes

  • Camera: the hero's screen position never moves — a scrolling cameraX slides the world past it instead
  • Parallax: far mountains, mid hills, and near hills scroll at different speeds for depth
  • Spawning: platforms, mushrooms, and grumps generate just ahead of the camera and are cleared once they scroll off-screen
  • Stomp check: landing on a grump from above defeats it; touching it any other way costs your growth (if big) or a heart (if small), plus a moment of invincibility
  • Win / lose state: reaching the flag or running out of hearts freezes the world, plays a short reaction, then shows your run's stats — no page reload needed to try again

Beyond the tutorial

The roadmap is complete, and since then: a 3-heart lives system with a real game-over state, synthesized sound effects (no external files needed), touch controls, and a gentle difficulty ramp the farther you run. Next up: a second level with a harder layout, or swapping the hand-drawn shapes for real sprite art.

No sprites, no game engine — just the 2D canvas API and hand-drawn shapes.
